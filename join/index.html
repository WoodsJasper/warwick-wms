Frequently asked questions

Membership questions

Contact WMS on Instagram for further information.

Where should I start?+

Join the WMS WhatsApp community and follow @warwick.wms on Instagram. These are the direct routes to society announcements, event details and conversations.

What can I learn with WMS?+

WMS explores wealth management through investment, asset classes, economic affairs, estate planning and tax planning. Speaker events, workshops and technical sessions connect those subjects with perspectives from the industry.

How can I get involved in the team?+

The official Linktree carries role information for Careers, Partnerships, Research / Portfolio Project, Sponsorship, Speakers and Marketing, alongside fund role descriptions. Check the latest society announcements for deadlines and whether applications are available.

Where can I confirm membership details?+

Contact WMS through the official Instagram account for the current membership arrangements and any fees. The society’s official channels are the place to confirm the details before you join an activity.

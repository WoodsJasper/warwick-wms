Fireside Chat with Mark Slater

A fireside chat with the Chairman and CIO of Slater Investments on growth investing, market cycles and his career.

Date
26 October 2026
Time
14:00–15:00 (UK local time)
Location
S0.21, University of Warwick

Warwick Wealth Management Society joins Warwick Hedge Fund Society and MFC Society for a fireside chat with Mark Slater, Chairman and Chief Investment Officer of Slater Investments.

The discussion explores growth investing, market cycles and his experience chairing the Dyson family office, with an opportunity to bring questions for the Q&A.

Careers / Past highlight

Finance 101

A careers panel with six Warwick finance societies covering different routes into the industry.

Date
08 October 2026
Time
13:00–14:00 (UK local time)
Location
FAB 0.03, University of Warwick

A careers panel bringing together Warwick Wealth Management Society, Warwick Private Equity, Warwick Investment Banking, Warwick Quant, Warwick Hedge Fund Society and Warwick Trading Society.

Students from across these societies shared perspectives on recruitment, industry knowledge, key skills and their own application experiences. The panel explored the different routes into deals, markets, investing, quantitative strategies and wealth management.

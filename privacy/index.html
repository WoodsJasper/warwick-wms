Privacy notice

What we collect, why we use it, who receives it and how to exercise your privacy rights.

✳

Effective 4 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-04-members

This notice covers the public Warwick WMS website at warwickwms.co.uk and its separate member service at warwick-wms.alwaysdata.net/warwick-wms. Account information is entered on the member service, not the public GitHub Pages website. Please read this notice before registering or sharing material.

Who is responsible

Data controller and website operator: Jasper Woods, operating this WMS website and member portal.
Privacy, account and complaint contact: jaspersbusinessstuff@gmail.com.

The operator decides how information is used for this service. This is a separately operated society platform; the University of Warwick and Warwick Students’ Union are not represented as its data controller. Their own services have separate notices. Contact the operator if you need help, further contact details or information about a data-sharing arrangement.

01

Information and where it comes from

When you register, you provide your name, seven-digit Warwick student ID and a portal password. The ID forms your Warwick email address, u[student ID]@live.warwick.ac.uk, which you must verify. We store account and verification status, a password hash, role and department, terms acceptance and its date, communication choices and the dates and wording versions recorded for those choices. We never ask for your University password or obtain your academic record.

If a society administrator invites or appoints you, we receive your name, university contact details and appointment information from that administrator or society appointment records. You can ask us about that source. Mailbox verification establishes access to that inbox; it does not establish current enrolment, an official society membership or a committee appointment.

The member directory shows active, email-verified members’ names, roles and departments to other authorised members, including profiles still completing setup. It does not show student IDs or email addresses. Messages record participants, encrypted text, timestamps, read positions and block choices. A report contains an encrypted copy of the selected message, the explanation, account references and review status.

Shared content includes files and their titles, descriptions, filenames, folders, sizes, dates, authorship and access permissions, together with announcements, published events and learning material. Authentication and security records include sessions, expiry, request and activity metadata, permission changes and access/audit events. Hosting suppliers also receive technical connection information such as IP addresses, requested addresses, browser information and timestamps.

Provide only your own accurate account information and material you are entitled to share. Do not upload unnecessary student lists, identification documents, sensitive personal information or confidential third-party material.

02

Purposes and lawful bases

We use the information needed to create and administer the account, verify its email address and provide the member content, conversations and resources you request on the basis of performing the member-service agreement. Required account fields are necessary to provide that verified service; without them, we cannot create an account. Public pages remain available without registering.

We rely on legitimate interests in operating a safe, useful society platform for proportionate security, access management, a discoverable member directory, moderation, service-message notifications and investigating misuse. We limit directory fields, restrict access, provide block/report tools and let you turn message emails off. We weigh these interests against members’ privacy and you may object; we will consider your circumstances and any overriding lawful reason to continue.

Optional promotional announcement emails and any future optional event emails rely on your separate consent. Refusing or withdrawing consent does not prevent account access. Agreement to the terms is not consent to marketing. We use information necessary to meet a specific legal duty, including handling data-protection rights and complaints, on the basis of that legal obligation.

We do not sell member contact details or give sponsors a member mailing list for their own marketing. We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects; administrators assign society roles and can review access or moderation decisions.

03

Account emails and optional notifications

Verification, invitation and password-reset emails support the account service. Google’s Gmail service handles the recipient address, message content and time-limited setup or recovery link. Verification links expire after one hour and password-reset links after 30 minutes. Never share these links or your password.

Message emails are service notifications, enabled by default after accepting the current terms, and can be switched off in account settings. They include the sender’s display name and full unread message text, sometimes grouped together, plus a link to the conversation. Gmail and the recipient’s mailbox service process that readable text; it can appear in inbox previews. Blocking, read state and account eligibility are checked before delivery. Turning these emails off leaves website chat available.

Announcement emails are optional and off until you make a separate, recorded choice to receive them. They may include society event promotion, learning opportunities and the full text of new public, member or team notices that your account may read. Old default-enabled settings do not count as a fresh opt-in. Access, current terms and your choice are checked before sending; historical posts, edits and republication do not create a new campaign. You can withdraw in account settings or email the contact above to unsubscribe without losing noticeboard access. Each announcement email identifies WMS and provides a contact/settings route.

Event email campaigns are not currently sent. Their separate preference does not activate a sender. Before introducing them we will explain the content, honour your choice and provide an easy unsubscribe. Consent withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Already delivered emails and copies retained by recipients cannot be recalled or deleted from their mailboxes by WMS.

04

Access, suppliers and overseas processing

Other members receive the directory and messages addressed to them. Administrators manage accounts, permissions, shared content and reported messages. Assigned staff receive the team/role access appropriate to their appointment. Administrators do not have an ordinary screen for browsing other people’s conversations; reporting deliberately shares the selected message and explanation with them. We may disclose necessary information to professional advisers, authorities or others where lawfully required or necessary to protect rights, security or people, rather than routinely sharing the database.

GitHub: the public visitor website is hosted on GitHub Pages. That host serves visitor files and processes technical requests under the GitHub privacy statement. It receives no WMS account forms, member database, private messages or resource files. Member links navigate to the separate member service.

Alwaysdata: the member application and persistent account database are hosted in the operator’s private Alwaysdata account in France. Alwaysdata provides hosting under its published hosting terms, including its customer-data processing terms. France is covered by the UK’s adequacy arrangements for EEA countries.

Google LLC: Gmail delivers account and notification emails; private Google Drive stores encrypted resource files with opaque names. File titles, descriptions, audiences and the decryption key stay with the application. Google receives encrypted file bytes, size and technical metadata, and readable email content for delivery. These are consumer Google services governed by Google’s privacy notice; Google acts as an independent controller for those services. We do not describe them as a Google Workspace processor agreement.

Suppliers and recipients may process information outside the UK, including the United States. Google explains its international-transfer safeguards, including the UK Extension to the Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers and contractual safeguards where applicable. The supplier notices explain their locations, disclosures, safeguards and how to obtain further details. Contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your data. A French application host does not mean all processing stays in France.

05

How information is protected

The member service uses HTTPS, private persistent storage, password hashing and server-side account, role, department and folder checks. Resource files and chat/report bodies have additional application encryption. The whole database is not separately encrypted by the application. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted: the server handles readable text, and a trusted operator with the keys can decrypt stored content. Approved recipients can retain their own downloaded or emailed copies.

Private data and credentials are excluded from the public build and source repository. Recovery copies are kept privately; operator-created database snapshots are encrypted and restore checked. These controls reduce risk but cannot promise that every breach, loss or interruption is impossible. Report suspected compromise or an unintended disclosure to the contact above promptly.

06

Retention and account closure

We keep information for the purposes described here, not indefinitely merely because storage is available. Retention is reviewed manually by the operator at least once each academic term, at committee handover and when an account closes or a relevant request is received. There is no general automated age-based deletion system. The following criteria guide that review:

  • Accounts and membership: while needed for an active or authorised society account. Unverified, abandoned and no-longer-needed accounts are considered for removal during review; role access is removed when an appointment ends. Necessary follow-up or a specific security/rights issue can justify limited, restricted retention.
  • Conversations: while needed by their participants for the society service. Deleting either participant’s account removes that direct conversation from the active database. Material reported for moderation is assessed separately.
  • Shared material: while the society has a continuing, permitted use for it. Account deletion does not automatically remove shared files, notices or events; authorship references are detached where supported. Contact us about material or personal information that should be removed.
  • Reports, security and delivery/choice records: only while needed to resolve an issue, prevent recurring misuse, administer delivery/preferences, demonstrate a choice or deal with an identifiable legal obligation or claim. Review considers the issue’s status, severity, recurrence risk and applicable legal time limits; longer retention is limited to the necessary evidence.
  • Recovery copies: for disaster recovery and any justified incident need. Alwaysdata’s Free plan has three rolling days of provider backups; these do not include separate Drive objects. Operator snapshots and independently backed-up resource objects are reviewed after releases and during termly review, retaining only copies needed for tested recovery or a specific open issue. A recovery operation must reapply relevant deletions and restrictions.

Non-administrators can delete their account in settings after confirming their password; administrators need an orderly handover. Deletion removes the login account, associated membership and linked conversations. Report copies may remain with account references cleared, but their text can still identify someone. Audit target references, shared content, necessary records, recipient mailbox copies and backups are not all erased immediately. Issued time-limited links may remain until expiry. Contact us for a wider erasure request or an explanation of any justified retained data.

07

Cookies, storage and external links

The public visitor site uses no WMS authentication cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The member service uses necessary first-party sign-in cookies, including the secure WMS session-token and sign-in preference cookies. They contain authentication identifiers, not your readable password, and are restricted to the member application.

The current password login creates a server-side session lasting up to 24 hours. Its browser-session cookies are normally removed when the browser session ends, although browser restoration settings can preserve them. Signing out invalidates the current session. Expiry makes a session or link unusable; it does not mean every associated database or security record is immediately deleted.

External links such as Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp take you to services with their own practices. Optional tracking or embedded third-party services will need appropriate information and controls before being introduced.

08

Your rights, requests and complaints

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, a portable copy. Rights depend on the circumstances. Account settings provide a useful JSON export and closure tools; they do not limit your right to ask about other personal information we hold.

Your right to object: you may object to processing based on legitimate interests because of your particular situation. You have an unconditional right to object to direct marketing at any time. You can also withdraw optional email consent in settings or by emailing us, without losing account access. We will stop direct marketing covered by your objection.

Email jaspersbusinessstuff@gmail.com with your request or privacy complaint. We may reasonably verify identity before disclosing or changing information. We respond to rights requests without undue delay, normally within one calendar month; where a lawful extension is needed, we explain it within the initial period. Any permitted timing adjustment will be explained.

We acknowledge data-protection complaints within 30 days, investigate, keep you informed and explain the outcome without undue delay. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Contacting us first can help resolve the issue, but does not remove your legal rights.

09

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when the service or use of information changes, identify the effective version and explain material changes through the service or an appropriate account communication. A new purpose or optional marketing choice will be assessed separately; acceptance of updated terms does not create marketing consent.

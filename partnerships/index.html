Course initiative · In development

Building towards a course with CAIA.

WMS is pursuing the development of a course with the CAIA Association, with the ambition of expanding learning opportunities for society members.

Alternative investment education

CAIA is an association focused on investment education, including alternative investments and their role in portfolios. The society’s proposed course remains in development.

The course format, syllabus, launch date and any certification arrangements are to be confirmed. Enrolment is not yet open.

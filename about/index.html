Warwick Wealth Management Society

About the society

Founded in 2023, WMS connects Warwick students with wealth management through speaker events, workshops and research.

View eventsJoin WMS

Our purpose

Education and industry connections

WMS supports students in developing their understanding of wealth management and the financial industry.

The society’s interests span investment strategies, asset classes and economic affairs, alongside estate and tax planning. Speaker events, workshops and technical sessions connect these subjects with industry experience.

Members can develop professional skills, meet other Warwick students and explore potential careers in finance.

Society activities
